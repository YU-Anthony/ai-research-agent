# Nearest-review overlap and provisional positioning

## 1. Braun, Weber and Alt: affective automotive UI review

Verified record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13731v2 . The retrieved full text identifies ACM Computing Surveys 54(7), Article 137, 2021, DOI 10.1145/3460938. The arXiv first-post year is 2020; use the verified publication year/version appropriately.

Targeted full-text inspection: Introduction describes systematic searches of ACM/Elsevier/IEEE/Springer plus Google Scholar, a query yielding 3,633 matches and subsequent relevance selection, deduplication and reference snowballing. These are **that paper's reported counts**, not our search counts. Sections 3-7 cover effects, emotion triggers, emotion-regulation evaluations, industry applications and methodology. It explicitly discusses paternalizing interactions and intervention timing.

Implication: "recognition to regulation", "emotion-aware car assistance" and "when to intervene" are not new simply by being proposed again. A defensible update needs generative-system evidence, a distinct evaluation question and explicit overlap with this review. Its reference list is a valuable backward-search source, not a substitute for reading the original studies.

## 2. Halin and colleagues: driver monitoring survey/synthesis

Verified record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.00472v1 . Targeted sections describe a systematic survey and a structured relationship among driver substates, indicators and sensing methods. It establishes substantial prior organization of recognition/state-monitoring evidence. Our potential focus is how driver-support claims are evaluated after inference, not a competing generic sensor taxonomy. Exact journal metadata should be reconciled when Crossref/publisher access is restored.

## 3. User-supplied HVI/AI collaboration review

Verified record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08005v1 . The HTML text was read. It covers human-in-the-loop cognition, trust, feedback, handover, workload, style, ethics, emotion/fatigue, and a SIDA framework. This is broad conceptual overlap. This v1 did not present a separately identifiable reproducible search/screening protocol in the sections inspected; do not infer that no other version or supplementary protocol exists. Do not treat its broad framework as experimental validation.

## 4. Ethical multimodal driver-monitoring review-to-design paper

Verified record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06439v1 . Abstract-only comparator in this pilot: regulation, consent, fairness, transparency and emotional well-being. Full-text eligibility and exact overlap with our measurement questions remain unchecked. A consent/ethics taxonomy alone would not sufficiently distinguish a new review.

## Working differentiation, not a novelty claim

Concentrate on evaluation of **generative driver-support assistants**: the relationship between upstream multimodal evidence and downstream measured outcomes; synthetic pairing versus naturally synchronized recordings; message/LLM-judge metrics versus human-state or driving outcomes; and user preference/control versus inferred emotion. Include a historical comparison set without claiming that older work lacks intervention evidence.

No complete ACM/IEEE/interdisciplinary search has run. Recent dedicated reviews may invalidate this positioning. The decision to proceed to a full manuscript should follow restored database access, systematic screening and human checking of the pilot.
