# Manuscript author-review checklist

## What has been written

- Full working English manuscript (`manuscript.md`) with abstract, introduction, scope, actual search method, four-layer synthesis, support/intervention evidence, evaluation-method evidence, discussion, agenda, limitations, conclusion and selected references.
- Chinese abstract and reading notes (`manuscript-zh-notes.md`).
- Selected versioned source registry (`manuscript-sources.json`) and BibTeX (`manuscript-references.bib`).
- Citation/registry integrity checking, separate from scientific verification.

## Mandatory completion gates

- [ ] Confirm the target research question and scope after nearest-review comparison.
- [ ] Restore database access and run complete, dated retrieval/citation searches; do not rebrand capped exploratory searches as exhaustive.
- [ ] Finish eligibility screening and publication/study-family reconciliation.
- [ ] Human-check each factual statement and interpretation against the exact source version.
- [ ] Resolve the Road to Serenity sample discrepancy and extract endpoint-specific denominators.
- [ ] Check participant allocation, conditions, instruments, uncertainty and disclosed interests before any stronger effectiveness claim.
- [ ] Decide which comparison publications belong in the final manuscript; candidate bibliography is not an inclusion list.
- [ ] Validate the four-layer framework against existing reviews and seek independent subject-matter critique.
- [ ] Replace the provisional abstract/counts/discussion after final corpus selection.
- [ ] Verify final publisher metadata, DOI, reference formatting and availability of legally reusable materials.
- [ ] Supply real author information, contributions, funding and competing-interest declarations. Do not invent them.
- [ ] Check the chosen venue's AI-assistance policy and complete author accountability review.
- [ ] Obtain explicit final-manuscript submission authorization. No authorization is implied by writing or GitHub storage.

Passing automated checks only demonstrates internal record/citation consistency. It does not establish completeness, novelty, correct interpretation, peer review or submission readiness. No publication venue or acceptance is promised.
